# Economy & Policy alternative — PIB RSS

When Marketaux's usage licence cannot be confirmed, use the independent **Discover → Economy & Policy** feed sourced from the Government of India's Press Information Bureau (PIB).

- Published PIB RSS index: https://www.pib.gov.in/ViewRss.aspx?lang=1&reg=3
- English releases feed: https://pib.gov.in/RssMain.aspx?ModId=6&Lang=1&Regid=3
- PIB copyright policy: https://www.pib.gov.in/content/102_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx

**This is financial/economic government news, NOT NSE/BSE stock prices or complete company-news coverage.** It selects only recent press releases with relevant economy/finance/business keywords. Some results may be tangential; all require editor verification. Headlines and a short summary are shown, always with PIB attribution and a link to the original.

The PIB policy permits reproduction of its own material without advance approval if it is represented accurately, not used in a misleading context, and **prominently attributes the source**. This does not cover any third-party copyrighted material embedded in PIB releases; do not reuse third-party text/photos unless separately authorised.

Technical: read-only, fixed RSS endpoint, no dynamic URL inputs, up to 1.5 MB XML, 8.5-second timeout, 15-minute in-memory cache, no source credentials, no Marketaux API calls. If PIB RSS is unreachable, the app reports an error and links to PIB's site rather than fabricating articles. Main RBI/SEBI polling remains separate.

If stock-specific corporate announcements are required, use the external NSE corporate-filings website link in the Indian Markets panel. NSE allows RSS subscriptions for news-reading, but its general terms also restrict automated collection and redistribution: **do not scrape or republish into SignalDesk without verifying rights**.

Marketaux remains a separately gated optional integration and will stay inactive unless permitted commercial reuse is confirmed; the new PIB source does not change or bypass its permission settings.
