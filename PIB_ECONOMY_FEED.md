# Economy & Policy alternative — PIB RSS

The independent **Discover → Economy & Policy** feed is sourced from the Government of India's Press Information Bureau (PIB). It complements RBI/SEBI and the manually curated MarketsMojo POC.

- Published PIB RSS index: https://www.pib.gov.in/ViewRss.aspx?lang=1&reg=3
- English releases feed: https://pib.gov.in/RssMain.aspx?ModId=6&Lang=1&Regid=3
- PIB copyright policy: https://www.pib.gov.in/content/102_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx

**This is financial/economic government news, NOT NSE/BSE stock prices or complete company-news coverage.** It selects only recent press releases with relevant economy/finance/business keywords. Some results may be tangential; all require editor verification. Headlines and a short summary are shown, always with PIB attribution and a link to the original.

The PIB policy permits reproduction of its own material without advance approval if it is represented accurately, not used in a misleading context, and **prominently attributes the source**. This does not cover any third-party copyrighted material embedded in PIB releases; do not reuse third-party text/photos unless separately authorised.

Technical: read-only, fixed RSS endpoint, no dynamic URL inputs, up to 1.5 MB XML, 8.5-second timeout, 15-minute in-memory cache, no source credentials, no proprietary news API calls. If PIB RSS is unreachable, the app reports an error and links to PIB's site rather than fabricating articles. Main RBI/SEBI polling remains separate.

For stock-specific corporate announcements, consult the official NSE corporate-filings website or the original company announcement, verifying reuse rights before automation. NSE allows RSS subscriptions for news-reading, but its general terms also restrict automated collection and redistribution: **do not scrape or republish into SignalDesk without verifying rights**.

MarketsMojo POC stories are manually curated and not a live feed. PIB does not depend on that demonstration workflow.
